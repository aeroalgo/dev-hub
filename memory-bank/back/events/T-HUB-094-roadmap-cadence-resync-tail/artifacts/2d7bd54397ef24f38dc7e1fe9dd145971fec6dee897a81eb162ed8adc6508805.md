## 1. QA Root Cause Analysis
During BACK QA review of epic `T-HUB-094-roadmap-cadence-resync-tail`, 1 blocker was identified:
- **BF-001 (B1 / ac_gap):** In `loop/roadmap_cadence.py:747` and `loop/roadmap_queue.py:1046`, when reconcile detects raw HIGH drift (`high_count > 0`), it creates structured evidence with `resync_action = "drift_detected"` and lists the affected epic IDs in `stale_epics`. In `loop/roadmap_queue.py:1046`, the resume gate expression previously treated a non-empty `stale_epics` list as confirmation of handled drift or allowed `is_resynced` to be true when `resync_action` was still `"drift_detected"`, allowing feature epics to resume on unhandled raw HIGH drift.

---

## 2. Changes Implemented (AC+)
- `loop/roadmap_queue.py`: Fixed `is_resynced` evaluation in `roadmap_advance` to strictly check `bool(ev_action and ev_action not in ("drift_detected", "unresynced")) or (has_evidence and ev_high == 0)`. When `ev_high > 0` and `ev_action in ("drift_detected", "unresynced")`, `is_resynced` is `False`, ensuring that raw HIGH drift fail-closed pauses feature advance.
- `loop/roadmap_cadence.py`: In `record_resync_evidence`, set `resync_action = "reconciled"` when `high_count == 0` and `"drift_detected"` when `high_count > 0`, correctly recording drift findings without treating raw drift as resolved.
- `loop/tests/test_roadmap_cadence.py`: Added test coverage verifying that `record_resync_evidence` sets `resync_action = "drift_detected"` when `high_count > 0`.
- `loop/tests/test_roadmap_queue.py`: Added `test_roadmap_advance_resync_raw_high_drift_blocks_resume` to explicitly verify that raw HIGH drift with `high_count > 0` and `resync_action="drift_detected"` remains fail-closed blocked by `roadmap_advance`.
- `memory-bank/back/bugfix/T-HUB-094-roadmap-cadence-resync-tail/bugfix-queue.yaml`: Completed BF-001 item with status `done`, targeted green evidence, and full test suite verification `pass`.
