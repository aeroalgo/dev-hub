# Bugfix: Roadmap Cadence Resume Gate on Raw HIGH Drift

**Epic ID:** T-HUB-094-roadmap-cadence-resync-tail

**Author:** BACK BUGFIX  
**Source:** memory-bank/back/qa/T-HUB-094-roadmap-cadence-resync-tail/qa-20260913-roadmap-cadence-resync-tail.yaml

## 1. QA Root Cause Analysis
During QA review (B1 / `ac_gap`), it was discovered that raw HIGH drift could bypass the resume gate in `roadmap_advance`:
- In `loop/roadmap_cadence.py:747` (`run_cadence_resync`), when reconcile detects HIGH drift (`high_count > 0`), it creates structured evidence with `resync_action="drift_detected"` and lists the affected epic IDs in `stale_epics`.
- In `loop/roadmap_queue.py:1046`, the resume gate check for `is_resynced` included `or (has_evidence and bool(getattr(ev, "stale_epics", None) or (isinstance(ev, dict) and ev.get("stale_epics"))))`.
- Because `run_cadence_resync` populated `stale_epics` with the detected drift findings, `stale_epics` was non-empty, causing `is_resynced` to evaluate to `True` even when `resync_action == "drift_detected"` and `high_count > 0`. This allowed feature epics to resume on unhandled raw HIGH drift.

---

## 2. Changes Implemented
- `loop/roadmap_queue.py`:
  - Fixed `is_resynced` calculation to rely strictly on `ev_action` not being in `("drift_detected", "unresynced")` (or `ev_high == 0`).
  - Removed `stale_epics` presence from the `is_resynced` boolean expression: `stale_epics` records diagnostic findings, not proof of resolved drift.
- `loop/tests/test_roadmap_cadence.py`:
  - Updated `test_advance_blocked_resync_high_drift` to explicitly verify that raw HIGH drift with `stale_epics=["T-FEAT-3"]` and `resync_action="drift_detected"` remains fail-closed blocked by `roadmap_advance`.
